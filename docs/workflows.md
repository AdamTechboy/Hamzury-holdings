# Operational Workflows

## 1. Customer Acquisition Pipeline
1. **Discovery**: Visitor explores Holding or Venture pages, selects an offer or initiates guided discovery.
2. **Submission**: Public enquiry submitted via form; validated and assigned a unique reference.
3. **Routing**: Department and available representative identified; email and WhatsApp notification dispatched.
4. **Qualification**: CRM Representative conducts discovery call/chat, updating lead status.
5. **Proposal & Quotation**: Itemized quote generated; sent to client portal with expiration date.

## 2. Sales Pipeline Stages
`NEW ENQUIRY` ➔ `CONTACTED` ➔ `QUALIFIED` ➔ `DISCOVERY` ➔ `PROPOSAL SENT` ➔ `NEGOTIATION` ➔ `WON / LOST / NURTURE`

## 3. Project Delivery Pipeline Stages
`AWAITING ACTIVATION` ➔ `ONBOARDING` ➔ `REQUIREMENTS PENDING` ➔ `READY FOR WORK` ➔ `IN PROGRESS` ➔ `QUALITY REVIEW` ➔ `AWAITING CLIENT APPROVAL` ➔ `DELIVERED` ➔ `AFTERCARE`

## 4. WhatsApp Integration Stages
- **Stage 1 (Click-to-chat)**: Pre-formatted links encoding enquiry reference and service title.
- **Stage 2 (Business API)**: Webhook ingestion of client replies linked by contact phone or lead reference.
- **Stage 3 (Shared Inbox)**: Multi-agent routing, conversation history, and internal notes.

## 5. Retention & Aftercare
Post-delivery triggers automatically log reviews, schedule renewal dates, and recommend cross-venture expansion (e.g. Website launch on SUBT ➔ Marketing kit on Medialy).
