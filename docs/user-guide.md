# Hamzury Platform User Guide

## For Staff Members

### 1. Accessing the Staff Workspace
- Navigate to `/staff/sign-in` and enter authorized staff credentials.
- Your dashboard displays:
  - Lead Queue: New and active enquiries assigned to you or your department.
  - Active Projects: Tasks awaiting your review or completion evidence.
  - Internal Tools:
    - **Item Saver** (`/staff/item-saver`): Search, categorize, and archive company knowledge links.
    - **Account Access Register** (`/staff/account-register`): Maintain custodian records and MFA verification. Never record passwords here.

### 2. Processing a Lead
1. Open the lead card from `/staff/crm`.
2. Review client assessment answers and submitted requirements.
3. Log calls or WhatsApp interactions using the activity logger.
4. Advance the pipeline stage to `DISCOVERY` or `PROPOSAL SENT`.
5. Click **Create Quotation** to generate an itemized quote.

---

## For Clients

### 1. Client Portal (`/portal`)
- View active projects, milestones, and task statuses in real-time.
- Upload requested documents (e.g. proof of identity, business registration, design assets).
- Review and accept itemized quotations.
- Request support or explore "Your Next Business Move" recommendations.
